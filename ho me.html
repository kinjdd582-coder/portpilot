Frequently Asked Questions
How do I connect a social media account?
Go to Connected Accounts and click the Connect button next to the platform you want to authorize. You'll be redirected through Zernio's OAuth flow and returned to PortPilot automatically.
What AI tools are available?
PortPilot includes AI tools for generating titles, captions, hashtags, CTAs, improving captions, rewriting captions, and generating thumbnail prompts. These are powered by OpenAI and require an active subscription.
How do I upgrade my plan?
Navigate to Billing, review the available plans, and click the Get Started or Upgrade button. You'll be directed to Flutterwave's secure checkout to complete the payment.
Can I schedule posts in advance?
Yes. In the Create studio, after composing your post, click "Schedule" and choose your desired publish date and time. Scheduled posts are stored in Firestore and published automatically via Zernio when the time arrives.
Where is my data stored?
All user data is stored in Firebase Firestore. Media assets are stored via Cloudinary. Payment and subscription data is managed by Flutterwave. No third party has access to your credentials.
